About

Two markets.
One marketer.

I trained as a computer scientist, then walked into marketing — and never really left the engineer's mindset behind. I started at Tsinghua University, producing campaigns that reached 10M+ views on a limited budget and supporting national exhibitions, including the Tsinghua Painting Annual Conference.

At Chinese Weekly in Beijing, I generated 10M+ impressions across print and digital, built cultural partnerships with the British Museum and Tate, and ran advertising programs for La Mer, Gucci, and Chanel. Then at SIA International Art Education, I directed content and growth across 37 social media accounts, built a 35-person team, lifted engagement by 300% — and saw that marketing contribute to $3M in signed contracts, paid media running alongside organic growth.

In parallel, I built my own thing: a consulting practice and a content audience of 200,000+ followers, advising 200+ paying clients one-on-one on art, culture, and personal growth. No agency, no safety net — just writing, trust, and results.

Then I crossed the ocean. In the US I ran a restaurant's operations and social media in Mesa, Arizona, and earned my MBA at Arizona State University (Class of 2026, GPA 3.86) — adding Python, R, SQL, and Tableau to a marketer's toolkit. I approach campaigns the way an engineer approaches a system: define the metric, design the experiment, read the data, iterate.

My purpose is to help brands — and people — cross borders: Chinese brands finding their American customers, and women building the skills to start a new chapter.
